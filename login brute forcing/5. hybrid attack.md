## Hybrid Attacks & Credential Stuffing: Optimized Notes

### 1. Hybrid Attacks (Logic + Guesswork)

A hybrid attack combines a **Dictionary Attack** with **Brute Force rules**. It targets the common human habit of making simple modifications to passwords to meet security policies.

- **The Logic:** Users often take a base word and add predictable elements like `!`, `@`, or the current year (e.g., `Summer2026!`).
    
- **The Method:** A tool takes a wordlist (like `rockyou.txt`) and automatically applies "rules" to every word—appending numbers, changing case, or swapping letters for symbols (e.g., `a` to `@`).
    

---

### 2. Strategic Wordlist Filtering

Efficiency comes from narrowing your wordlist to match the target's **Password Policy**. You can use `grep` with Regular Expressions (Regex) to filter a massive list into a small, high-probability set.

**Example: Filter for 8+ characters, 1 Uppercase, 1 Lowercase, and 1 Number:**

Bash

```
grep -E '^.{8,}$' list.txt | grep -E '[A-Z]' | grep -E '[a-z]' | grep -E '[0-9]' > filtered.txt
```

- **Impact:** This can turn a 10,000-word list into <100 high-quality guesses, making the attack faster and harder to detect.
    

---

### 3. Credential Stuffing (The Domino Effect)

This is not about guessing; it is about **reusing stolen data**.

1. **The Source:** Attackers obtain "Combo Lists" (Email:Password pairs) from previous data breaches (e.g., a LinkedIn or Adobe leak).
    
2. **The Attack:** An automated script "stuffs" these pairs into different sites (Bank, Social Media, VPN).
    
3. **The Vulnerability:** It relies entirely on **Password Reuse**. If a user uses the same password for a small forum as they do for work, one breach compromises both.
    

---

### 4. Summary Comparison

|**Attack Type**|**Core Strategy**|**Best Countermeasure**|
|---|---|---|
|**Hybrid**|Mutating words with patterns (e.g., `Word123!`).|**Long Passphrases** (harder to mutate).|
|**Stuffing**|Using leaked data from other sites.|**Multi-Factor Auth (MFA)** & unique passwords.|

---

### 5. Recon Application

- **Policy Identification:** Look for "Password must contain..." errors on login pages to build your `grep` filters.
    
- **Breach Checking:** Use OSINT tools to see if target emails appear in known leaks before starting an attack.
