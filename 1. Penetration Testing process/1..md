### Internal
- From within the corporate network.
- After external pentest.
---

## Types of Testing

### 1. Blackbox
- Minimal. Only the essential info such as IP address and domains is provided.

### 2. Greybox
- Extended. Additional info such as specific URLs , hostnames , subnets etc.

### 3. WHitebox
- Maximum. Everything disclosed. Internal view of entire structure. Detailed configurations, admin creds, web app souurce code , etc.

### 4. Red - Teaming
- May include physical testing and social engineering, among other things. Can be combined with any of above.

### 5. Purple Teaming
- Combined with any of the above. Focuses on working closely with the defenders.

### Pillaging
- After gaining initial access to system, collecting data to move further.

## Pre-Engagement
- Adjustment of contract with client such as 
    - Non - Disclosure Agreement.
    - Goals
    - Scope
    - Time Estimation
    - Rules of Engagement.

## Info Gathering
- Collecting info about the target company and the software and hardware in use to find potential security gaps to leverage foothold.

## Vulnerability Assessment
- Evaluation of potential vulnerabilities both manually and through automated means.
- Determine threat level and susceptibility of a company's network infrastructure to cyber- attacks.
## Exploitation
- Use results to test attack against potential vectors and execute to gain initail access to systems.

## Post-Exploitation
- Pivilege escilation to obtain highest possible rights and hunt for data such as credentials or other data.

## Lateral Movement
- Movement within the internal network of our  target company to access additional hosts at the same or a higher privilege level.
## Proof-of-Concept
- Document a clear, step-by-step path of how you chained weaknesses to gain access, so the client can understand impact and prioritize fixes.

## Post-Engagement
- Post-engagement involves preparing detailed reports for admins and management, cleaning up traces, delivering findings (reports/presentations), and archiving test data until retesting or per policy.
