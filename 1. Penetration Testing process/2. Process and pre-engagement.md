- **Non-Disclosure Agreement(NDA) must be signed by all parties.**

## Types of NDA

- **Unilateral NDA** – One-way confidentiality. Only one party’s (usually the client’s) information is protected. The tester must keep client data secret, but the client can share tester’s methods/findings.
    
- **Bilateral NDA** – Two-way confidentiality. Both parties protect each other’s information. Most common in pentesting since it protects both client data and tester’s work/reports.
    
- **Multilateral NDA** – More than two parties agree to confidentiality. Used when multiple organizations (e.g., partners in a cooperative network) are involved in the same test.

# 🛡️ Pre-Engagement Phase

## 1. **Authorization**

- Sign-off by: CEO / CTO / CISO / CSO / CIO / CRO / Audit / IT Sec / Internal Audit
    
- Define: signatory authority, POCs (primary/secondary), escalation contacts, tech support
    

---

## 2. **Key Docs & Timing**

- NDA → after initial contact
    
- Scoping Questionnaire → before pre-engagement meeting
    
- Scoping Document → during meeting
    
- SoW (Proposal/Contract) → during meeting
    
- Rules of Engagement (RoE) → before kick-off
    
- Contractors Agreement (physical tests) → before kick-off
    
- Reports → during & after test
    

---

## 3. **Scoping Questionnaire**

- Test type: VA / PT / Web / App / Wireless / Physical / Social Eng. / Red Team
    
- Scope: #IPs, domains, SSIDs, apps, roles, phishing targets, AD, NAC, etc.
    
- Style: Black / Grey / White box
    
- Evasiveness: Non-evasive / Hybrid / Fully evasive
    

---

## 4. **Pre-Engagement Meeting**

- Discuss: goals, scope, test details, methodology (OSSTMM, OWASP, PTES…)
    
- Locations: internal, external, VPN
    
- Risks & 3rd-party approvals
    
- Communication & escalation paths
    
- Output: Contract (SoW) + RoE
    

---

## 5. **Contract Checklist (SoW)**

- NDA signed
    
- Goals, Scope, Test Type, Methodology
    
- Time & Locations (internal/external, remote/local)
    
- 3rd-party approvals
    
- Risks, Limitations, Compliance reqs (HIPAA, PCI…)
    
- Contacts & comms
    
- Reporting format & payment terms
    

---

## 6. **Rules of Engagement (RoE)**

- Contractor & tester info
    
- Purpose, Goals, Scope (IPs, domains, systems)
    
- Time, duration, daily windows
    
- Testing type, methodology
    
- Objectives / flags
    
- Evidence, backup, incident handling
    
- Reporting (status + final), retesting
    
- Disclaimers, liability, signed permission
    

---

## 7. **Kick-Off Meeting**

- Formal start (after docs signed)
    
- Attendees: client POCs (Audit, IT, Sec), support staff, pentesters, PM
    
- Review scope, confirm **no DoS**
    
- Pause protocol for critical findings
    
- Risks: logs, alarms, lockouts, disruptions
    
- Adapt explanation to client maturity
    

---

## 8. **Contractors Agreement (Physical Tests)**

- Contractor info, purpose, goal
    
- Tester names + IDs
    
- Locations (building, floors, rooms)
    
- Timeline & notarization
    
- Signed permission to avoid legal issues
