| **Component**        | **Simple Role**    | **Analogy**                                                                                                    |
| -------------------- | ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **WinLogon**         | The Manager        | The person in charge of the whole lobby. They decide when to show the login screen and when to lock the doors. |
| **LogonUI**          | The Receptionist   | The actual screen you see. They ask for your "ID card" (password, PIN, or fingerprint).                        |
| **LSASS**            | The Security Guard | The "brain" of the operation. They take your password and check it against the records to see if it's real.    |
| **SAM**              | The Local Vault    | A locked database on your computer that holds all the local usernames and passwords.                           |
| **Active Directory** | The Main Office    | If you are at a big company, the "vault" isn't on your PC; it’s a giant server in the back office.             |
