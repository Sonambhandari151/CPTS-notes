
# 🧠 1. Concept (What & Why)

### What?

- **SocksOverRDP** creates a **SOCKS5 proxy over an RDP connection**
    
- Uses **RDP Dynamic Virtual Channels (DVC)**
    

### Why?

- When:
    
    - ❌ SSH not available
        
    - ❌ Cannot use chisel/dnscat
        
    - ✅ Only RDP access exists
        

👉 So we **abuse RDP to tunnel traffic**

# 🔥 5. Key Understanding (Very Important)

---

## ✔ Why 127.0.0.1?

- SOCKS proxy runs locally on pivot
    
- Proxifier sends traffic there first
    

---

## ✔ Why Proxifier?

- Windows apps don’t support SOCKS directly
    
- Proxifier forces routing
    

---

## ✔ Why two components?

|Component|Role|
|---|---|
|Plugin DLL|Creates tunnel|
|Server EXE|Forwards traffic|

---

## ✔ Why RDP tunneling?

- Works when:
    
    - Only RDP allowed
        
    - No outbound ports open
